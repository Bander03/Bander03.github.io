## Daily Search-AI Beat — 2026-09-17

**Why it matters:** AI Overviews' real reach is contested across trackers, and Google just gave
its AI-Mode agents a much bigger, dated rollout than most coverage mentions.

- Google rolled out AI Mode "information agents" — which monitor blogs, news, and social posts
  continuously — to Ultra subscribers on **June 12, 2026**. This is a specific, dated rollout,
  not a rumor.
- AI Overview prevalence is genuinely contested: BrightEdge's tracker shows ~48% of keywords,
  Semrush's panel peaked at ~24.6% (July 2025) before dropping to ~15.7% (November), and
  Conductor's Q1 2026 benchmark found ~25.1%. Three different trackers, three different numbers
  — treat any single "AI Overviews show up in X% of searches" claim as tracker-dependent, not a
  fixed fact.
- Query type matters far more than the headline percentage: comparison ("X vs Y") queries trigger
  AI Overviews ~95.4% of the time and question-format queries ~85.9%, vs. only ~8% for commercial
  and ~5% for transactional queries.

**Watch next:** whether the "information agents" rollout expands past Ultra subscribers — that's
the trigger that would make this relevant beyond a small subscriber tier.
